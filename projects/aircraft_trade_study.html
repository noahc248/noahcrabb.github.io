Overview

A flexible page for aircraft concept work

Since your homepage implied a fifth project but the repository only had one detail page, this page fills that gap with a clean aircraft-design project shell. It matches the rest of the site and gives you a place to document a sizing study without having to build another layout later.

You can treat this as a placeholder or turn it into a real project page by replacing the starter copy with your mission requirements, constraint plots, weight estimates, and final concept summary.

Suggested Content

What to drop into this page later

  • Mission definition, design requirements, and the performance goals that drove the concept selection.
  • Constraint analysis showing how stall speed, takeoff, climb, and cruise informed wing loading and thrust-to-weight choices.
  • Early aerodynamic estimates from XFLR5 or hand calculations, including lift curve, drag polar, or stability observations.
  • A short final section on what changed during the design loop and which tradeoffs mattered most.
Structure

How this page can evolve

Analysis section

Use one side of the page for sizing logic, equations, and performance plots. This is a good place for wing loading versus thrust-to-weight charts, mission fuel fractions, or summary tables from MATLAB and Excel.

Design section

Use the other side for the aircraft itself: layout sketches, geometry decisions, CAD renders, or notes on why one concept was chosen over another.
