Overview

What this project covered

This project centered on designing and refining a competition aircraft for Virginia Tech's 3D Printed Aircraft Competition team. The work combined concept development, detailed CAD, assembly planning, and repeated prototype improvements based on testing feedback.

My contribution focused on turning team design ideas into manufacturable geometry: building clean part models, coordinating interfaces between components, and helping the team move from screen to printed hardware as efficiently as possible.

Role

CAD, integration, and team coordination

  • Co-developed the aircraft CAD model with attention to fuselage layout, wing interfaces, and printable part breakdowns.
  • Supported assembly integration so airframe components could be manufactured, joined, and adjusted without slowing down the full team.
  • Worked through rapid prototype revisions in Fusion 360 and SOLIDWORKS based on test findings, print constraints, and performance goals.
  • Contributed to the competition-ready design that earned 1st Place for Most Innovative Design and 2nd Place in Time Aloft.
Process

How the design work moved forward

Digital design workflow

CAD work emphasized part organization, clean geometry, and practical interfaces so the aircraft could be revised quickly as competition priorities evolved. That meant modeling with downstream printing, fit-up, and assembly changes in mind from the start.

Build-test iteration

The team used a fast loop of printing, inspecting, assembling, and refining components. Each round of testing informed the next round of CAD changes, helping the design become more flight-ready without losing development speed.

Results

Why it matters

This project was a strong example of engineering under real constraints: limited time, physical manufacturing limits, team coordination, and the need to make design decisions that hold up in actual flight hardware. It strengthened my CAD workflow and reinforced how much good aircraft design depends on iteration, communication, and integration.
